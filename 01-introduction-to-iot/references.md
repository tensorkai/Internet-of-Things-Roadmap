# Sources and visual credits

[← Module home](README.md)

The lessons use APA 7th edition author–date citations linked to the entries below. Worked examples, sample requirements, exercises, numerical targets, and diagrams are original curriculum material unless otherwise stated. They do not reproduce a certified reference design.

## Technical references

<a id="ref-mqtt"></a>

Banks, A., Briggs, E., Borgendale, K., & Gupta, R. (Eds.). (2019, March 7). *MQTT version 5.0* [OASIS Standard]. OASIS. https://docs.oasis-open.org/mqtt/mqtt/v5.0/os/mqtt-v5.0-os.html

**Used for:** message delivery and acknowledgement semantics in lesson 2.

<a id="ref-lifecycle"></a>

Fagan, M., Megas, K. N., Cuthill, B., Marron, J., & Hoehn, B. (2026). *Foundational cybersecurity activities for IoT product manufacturers* (NIST IR 8259 Rev. 1). National Institute of Standards and Technology. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8259r1

**Used for:** product cybersecurity planning in lessons 5–6. This revision supersedes the 2020 NISTIR 8259 publication.

<a id="ref-security"></a>

Fagan, M., Megas, K. N., Scarfone, K., & Smith, M. (2020). *IoT device cybersecurity capability core baseline* (NISTIR 8259A). National Institute of Standards and Technology. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8259A

**Used for:** the six device capability areas in lesson 5.

<a id="ref-itu"></a>

International Telecommunication Union. (2012). *Overview of the Internet of things* (Recommendation ITU-T Y.2060). https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-Y.2060-201206-I/en

**Used for:** the introductory IoT model in lesson 1. The recommendation was renumbered Y.4000 in 2016 without substantive republication.

<a id="ref-edge"></a>

Iorga, M., Feldman, L., Barton, R., Martin, M. J., Goren, N. S., & Mahmoudi, C. (2018). *Fog computing conceptual model* (NIST Special Publication 500-325). National Institute of Standards and Technology. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.500-325

**Used for:** the placement of processing near devices and its relationship to remote services in lesson 3.

<a id="ref-gum"></a>

Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology. (2008). *Evaluation of measurement data—Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement* (JCGM 100:2008). https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM100-2008E

**Used for:** measurement uncertainty in lesson 4. The guide is a reference for later measurement work, not prerequisite reading in full.

<a id="ref-systems"></a>

National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (2016). *NASA systems engineering handbook* (Rev. 2, NASA/SP-2016-6105). https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf

**Used for:** requirements, verification, and validation concepts in lessons 4 and 6, especially Chapters 4–5.

<a id="ref-privacy"></a>

National Institute of Standards and Technology. (2020). *NIST privacy framework: A tool for improving privacy through enterprise risk management, version 1.0*. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.01162020

**Used for:** purpose, data processing, and privacy risk in lesson 5.

## Original graphics

The [module header](assets/module-header.svg) and Mermaid diagrams were created for this course. They are illustrations, not photographs. Their neutral surfaces, charcoal typography, and restrained green accents match the main roadmap's OpenAI-inspired design; the repository is an independent educational project.
